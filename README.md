# BubbleMap

A square minimap and a map window for World of Warcraft: Forever, part of the Bubble suite.

- **The minimap:** square, sized to fit; zooms far further in and out than the game's, drawing the real
  terrain (or the world map's painted art) itself. **Gather mode** shows the game's own minimap at its
  zoom levels for live herb and ore blips. A clean bar of buttons on top, and every addon's minimap
  button collected into one list.
- **The map window:** every continent on one map you can drag and zoom, from the whole world down to a
  few yards, in terrain or painted art (fully revealed or as explored). Zone names, the area under the
  cursor, flight masters, zone exits, dungeon entrances, party members with their names.
- **Waypoints:** click the map window to drop one; Shift+click shares it with party members who have
  BubbleMap.
- **With BubbleQuest:** its tracker hangs under the minimap as one column, its quest areas and guide line
  draw on both maps, and the map window gets its quest log as a drawer.

Open the map window with `/bmap map`, a key binding (Options > Keybindings > AddOns > BubbleMap), or a
right-click on the world map's BubbleMap button. Settings: Options > AddOns > BubbleMap. `/bmap` for
status.
