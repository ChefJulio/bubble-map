# BubbleMap 0.1.0 (beta)

First release, as its own addon (it grew up inside BubbleQuest's repository).

- Square minimap with its own map: the real terrain or the world map's painted art, zoom far beyond the
  game's, a zoom slider, rotation support.
- Gather mode: the game's minimap at its own zoom levels for live herb and ore blips.
- A bar of buttons on top (zone, gather, map style, tracking, calendar, mail, clock) and every addon's
  minimap button collected into one list.
- Map window: every continent on one map, drag and zoom, terrain or painted art (revealed or as
  explored), zone names, area names on hover, flight masters, zone exits, dungeon entrances, events,
  party members, coordinates.
- Waypoints, private or shared with party members who have BubbleMap.
- With BubbleQuest: the tracker under the minimap, quest areas and the guide line on both maps, the quest
  log as a drawer in the map window.
- Terrain on the world map's zone maps (optional).
